# 📄 Module 006 — Customer Discovery & Marketplace Browsing

## Overview
This module empowers prospective buyers and vehicle owners to explore featured top vehicles, search across showrooms, and dynamically filter vehicles and spare parts.

---

### US-006-01: Featured "Top Vehicles" Showcase
- **As a** Customer,  
- **I want to** see a "Top Vehicles" highlight section on the marketplace homepage,  
- **So that** I can quickly discover popular, trending, or featured bikes and cars.  
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  1. Displays high-resolution vehicle cards showing Brand, Model, Price, Engine CC, and Showroom Name.
  2. Clicking a vehicle card opens its detailed view page.

---

### US-006-02: Dynamic Multi-Attribute Vehicle Search & Filtering
- **As a** Customer,  
- **I want to** filter vehicles by Category (Bike/Car), Showroom, Brand, Price Range, Engine CC, and Color,  
- **So that** I can find vehicles matching my exact budget and performance preferences.  
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  1. Filters update results dynamically without requiring full page reloads.
  2. Multi-select capability allows combining filters (e.g., *Bike + Honda + Red + Under $3,000*).
  3. Clear "Reset Filters" button restores default catalog view.

---

### US-006-03: Spare Parts Public Marketplace Search
- **As a** Customer,  
- **I want to** search spare parts by Part Name, Vehicle Type (Bike/Car), or Showroom,  
- **So that** I can locate required replacement parts nearby.  
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  1. Part card shows Part Name, Price, Showroom Location, Compatibility tag, and Stock status badge.
  2. Detail page includes direct action buttons for enquiry or purchase request.
